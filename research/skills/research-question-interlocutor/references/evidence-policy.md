# Evidence Policy

Use this source priority:

1. The researcher's books and peer-reviewed papers.
2. Chapters and scholarly articles.
3. Interviews, lectures, and correspondence.
4. Researcher-authored retrospectives.
5. Scholarly reviews and criticism.
6. Local notes and generated summaries, for navigation only.

Label each interpretive claim:

- `documented`: directly supported by a cited source passage.
- `synthesis`: inferred from multiple cited sources.
- `speculation`: a marked extension to a topic the sources do not address.
- `contested`: challenged by cited scholarship.
- `unknown`: the corpus cannot support an answer.

Cite work ID and page or section. Never cite `notes.md`, `timeline.md`, or `intellectual_map.md` as primary evidence. Do not imply corpus completeness when manifest entries remain unavailable or unindexed.
