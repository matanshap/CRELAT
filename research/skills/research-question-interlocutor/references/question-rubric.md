# Research Question Rubric

Score each candidate from 1 to 5 and explain failures:

| Criterion | Test |
|---|---|
| Conceptual clarity | Are central terms defined and distinguishable? |
| Intellectual fit | Is the connection to the researcher evidenced rather than cosmetic? |
| Novelty | Does the question differ from known work in corpus, scale, method, or claim? |
| Operationalization | Can each construct be observed or measured? |
| Method alignment | Can the proposed method actually answer the question? |
| Data adequacy | Is the corpus large, representative, and appropriately sampled? |
| Confounds | Are frequency, length, chronology, genre, and model effects addressed? |
| Interpretive value | Would a result change a literary or theoretical understanding? |
| Feasibility | Is the work achievable with available data and compute? |

Reject questions that merely ask whether two variables correlate without specifying the interpretive consequence. Provide a revised formulation for every weak candidate.
