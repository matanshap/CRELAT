# Output Schema

Return:

1. **Working problem:** one paragraph defining the user's aim and constraints.
2. **Project direction fit:** how the answer is being constrained by
   `docs/research-direction.md`, including any inferred or missing direction.
3. **Researcher lens:** relevant documented commitments, tensions, and corpus limits.
4. **Candidate questions:** three to five materially different questions.
5. **Scrutiny matrix:** rubric scores, evidence, confounds, feasibility, project fit, and likely objections.
6. **Strongest revision:** one decision-ready question with units, corpus, comparison, and contribution.
7. **Evidence ledger:** claim label, work ID, page or section, and source path/URL.
8. **Unknowns:** missing evidence and acquisition or analysis needed next.
