# David Bamman: Provisional Intellectual Map

This map is deliberately provisional because no local full-text corpus has yet been supplied.

## Recurring problems to test against the sources

- How NLP methods can be adapted for cultural and literary domains that are poorly served by generic benchmarks.
- How annotation, dataset construction, and model design shape what can be claimed about literature, film, music, and social media.
- How computational methods can operationalize characters, places, narrative structure, representation, and cultural variation.
- How empirical cultural analysis should handle domain expertise, scale, and interpretability.
- How LLMs can be evaluated and constrained for cultural analytics rather than treated as neutral general-purpose readers.

## Productive tensions

- Domain-specific modeling versus general NLP architectures.
- Scalable cultural measurement versus interpretive validity.
- Dataset creation as scholarly argument versus dataset creation as infrastructure.
- Representation and fairness claims versus corpus and annotation limits.
- LLM capability versus cultural and sociolinguistic grounding.

Do not use these statements as evidence. Replace or refine them after indexed primary passages and critical reception have been reviewed.
