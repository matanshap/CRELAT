# Andrew Piper: Navigation Timeline

This file is a navigation aid. Verify interpretations against primary sources.

## 2006–2014: Books, media, and practices of reading

Work on Romantic print culture, bibliography, translation, reading technologies, and the embodied dimensions of books. Primary anchors: *Dreaming in Books* and *Book Was There*.

## 2015–2020: Computational modeling and textual evidence

Work increasingly joins literary interpretation to explicit models, larger corpora, fictionality, character, and questions of generalization. Primary anchors: “Novel Devotions,” “Fictionality,” “Think Small,” *Enumerations*, “Social Characters,” and *Can We Be Wrong?*.

## 2021–present: Narrativity, character, and AI

Work develops computational accounts of narrative discourse, fictional characters, narrative shape, social networks, datasets, and LLM-assisted analysis. Primary anchors: “Narrative Theory for Computational Narrative Understanding,” “Computational Narrative Understanding,” “What Do Characters Do?,” and “The Social Lives of Literary Characters.”

The forthcoming *Why You Should Read More Fiction* is metadata-only until publication and legal acquisition.
