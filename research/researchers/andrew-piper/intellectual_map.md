# Andrew Piper: Provisional Intellectual Map

This map is deliberately provisional because no local full-text corpus has yet been supplied.

## Recurring problems to test against the sources

- How material and technical forms organize reading.
- How literary categories can be translated into explicit, inspectable models.
- How quantitative evidence changes the scale and credibility of literary generalization.
- How fictional discourse, characters, and narrative patterns can be operationalized without exhausting their interpretive meaning.
- How datasets, annotation practices, and models participate in the claims they support.

## Productive tensions

- Generalization versus historical and textual specificity.
- Model legibility versus representational power.
- Repetition and aggregation versus singular reading experiences.
- Predictive performance versus interpretive consequence.
- Large-scale evidence versus corpus vulnerability and selection effects.

Do not use these statements as evidence. Replace or refine them after indexed primary passages and critical reception have been reviewed.
